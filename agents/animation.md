# Animation role

Input: accepted still look or explicitly labeled QA-preview permission, frame count/fps, bounded render budget.
Output: editable animation, short MP4, actual frame-count/decode checks and motion review evidence.

Inspect beginning, middle and end, then the moving preview. The liquid must rise continuously; the stream must connect its source to the receiving surface; the nozzle must have clearance. Check topology and surface contact numerically, then inspect visible temporal noise/flicker and continuity. Still images cannot establish motion quality.

The current two-key fixed-topology morph is an art-directed proxy. It does not solve viscosity, pressure, mass conservation or turbulence. Cloudy-to-clear comes only after the clear-pour look; do not attach chemical readiness claims to it.
